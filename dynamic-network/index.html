How to read this network · methods & limitations

Each edge joins a trial to an exact original reason label, summing positive counts only across that trial’s reconciled arms. Only usable trials with valid arm counts and reason sums equal to non-completions contribute. Zero counts are not edges. A missing edge is not evidence of zero withdrawals. No synonym merging or inferred relationships are used.

“Shared” labels occur in at least two trials in the full dated network; this fixed membership keeps the timeline comparable. Counts and node sizes update with the displayed time window. Counts reflect study size and reporting practices, not comparative risk. Overall trial attrition colors use all usable arms, while edges can represent only the subset of reconciled arms.

Time means trial completion year, not withdrawal date or publication date. Missing years are excluded and counted below. Force-layout distances have no analytical meaning. Positions persist between years; dragging pins a node until double-clicked. The existing source datasets are unchanged.

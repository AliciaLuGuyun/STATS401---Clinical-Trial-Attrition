      Methodology, limitations & reproducibility

      Source and scope

      ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API, retrieved September 13, 2026; source processing September 11. Four immutable local JSON pages, 335 candidate studies, 201 eligible studies. Completed interventional studies with Phase II/III present, randomized parallel design, structured results and explicit major-depression labels; mixed bipolar/schizophrenia labels deferred. This is a selected registry cohort, not a census. Field-level definitions.

      Rates, periods and mappings

      Attrition = 1 − COMPLETED/STARTED, with positive STARTED and valid participant counts; aggregate arm counts before dividing. STARTED is not automatically number randomized. Enrollment differs from STARTED in 11 usable trials and is never substituted. The sole reported period can include follow-up. 51 trials have multiple periods; 106 need protocol/flow arm-mapping review; these categories overlap. No ambiguous period is selected. Exact label matches allow only case/whitespace normalization and must form a bijection.

      Paired contrasts require exactly two groups, one explicitly EXPERIMENTAL and one explicit placebo, active, sham or no-intervention comparator. Registry types—not row order—define roles. NCT00149643 reports zero completions and is retained as a flagged anomaly, with a separate sensitivity analysis. Counts can be arithmetically valid without a resolved interpretation.

      Missingness and selection

      Missing values remain missing. Reasons are not synonym-merged; shares require the complete arm reason sum to reconcile with non-completions. 152 usable arms across 55 trials have reconciled reasons, while 69 otherwise usable arms lack a reason sum. Exact calendar span exists for only 39 usable trials and measures recruitment/study time, not participant follow-up. Sponsor extraction imbalance (52/155 INDUSTRY; 28/43 OTHER) limits generalization.

      Model and outcome limits

      The three-covariate, equal-trial regression estimates associations with overall trial attrition, not differential attrition, causes or predictions. All bootstrap coefficient intervals cross zero; the sponsor estimate is sensitive to the flagged record. HC3 standard errors and diagnostics are exported. We inspected 330 primary outcomes and 1,888 cells. The 14 MADRS-change candidates require adjudication of timepoint, analysis population, adjustment, missing-data handling and direction before an effect analysis. No effect-of-attrition or reporting-bias claim has been established.
